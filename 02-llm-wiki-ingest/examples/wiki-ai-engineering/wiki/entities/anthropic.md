# Anthropic

> The company behind MCP and Claude Code, and the source of both the protocol roadmap and the "connectivity is not one thing" framing the wiki keeps returning to.

## Definition

Anthropic appears in the sources as the originator of MCP — 18 months ago "just a
little spec document, a few SDKs, mostly written by Claude, local only" — and as
the party that added remote capabilities, centralized authorization, elicitation
and tasks on top of what the community built
[[wiki/sources/the-future-of-mcp-vs-skills]]. In the four-layer post it is cited
through David Soria Parra, an MCP co-creator
[[wiki/sources/the-future-of-mcp-why-the-future-of-agents-is-mcp-skills]].

## Key claims

- MCP began as a minimal local-only spec and grew into a protocol with remote transport, centralized authorization, elicitation and experimental applications. [[wiki/sources/the-future-of-mcp-vs-skills]]
- Anthropic ships progressive tool discovery in its own product and expects the pattern to be available on competitor APIs too. [[wiki/sources/the-future-of-mcp-vs-skills]]
- Protocol governance has moved to an open foundation with a public issue tracker and Discord, explicitly soliciting correction. [[wiki/sources/the-future-of-mcp-vs-skills]]
- The connectivity-stack argument that anchors this wiki is attributed to an Anthropic MCP co-creator. [[wiki/sources/the-future-of-mcp-why-the-future-of-agents-is-mcp-skills]]
- The house framing of the trajectory: 2024 was demos, 2025 was coding agents, 2026 is connectivity. [[wiki/sources/system-architecture-of-future-ai-apps-ui-tui-ide-extension]]
